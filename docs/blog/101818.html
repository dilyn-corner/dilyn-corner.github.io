It's been two months since my last update. I have a lot of things that I could

my lunch break is basically over.

I feel like we should answer those questions.

You keep apologizing to me about and for your ups and downs and how they can be
so extreme. But I'm here for it. I'm here for you. I'm in this for you. I want
all of you. I want everything about you. I don't know how to say it because we
never really established what we "are", but I want you. I want to be with you,
and make you happy, and smile and laugh and dance and enjoy life. I want this
for the long-term. You're too special and precious to me to let graduate school
whisk you away. You don't make me want to appy for Ph.D programs just to better
myself and get out of this town, but also because I want to be with you. I want
to go with you on this crazy adventure. I could follow you anywhere.

I don't know how else to say that. I hope you feel a similar way.

And I don't want you to pour yourself into me to make me full. It only really
works if we both make each other full. I don't know how to make you stop doing
that, though. It isn't that I need you to be happy - your presence in my life
alone makes me want to be happy. I quit smoking because I don't want to die
anymore. You make me not want to be dead. You make me want to be alive, and you
make me want to share my life and share experiences with other people. I won't
make you love me, but I'm gonna make you hate deciding not to.
